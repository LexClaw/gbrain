# Discover Crypto Assessment + Workbook Model (2026-06-11)

## Context

TJ asked for deep research and product architecture for Discover Crypto Skool member materials inspired by Dave Ramsey's Financial Peace University. Target audience: people who have bought Bitcoin or crypto but have no system, plus some traders and true beginners. Desired deliverables: a free assessment/scoring rubric and a paid post-signup workbook.

## Durable product lesson

Do not frame the product as another "learn crypto" course. The stronger Skool onboarding product is a behavior-change system:

> Build your crypto investing system in your first 7 days inside Discover Crypto.

The FPU pattern to borrow is not the finance doctrine. It is the structure:

1. Diagnose the member before prescribing.
2. Give a simple named path.
3. Make them write down numbers, goals, holdings, and rules.
4. Gate advanced/risky behavior behind foundation completion.
5. Use community accountability to reinforce the system.

## Recommended free front-door

**Crypto Investor Readiness Assessment**

Purpose: lead capture, segmentation, and onboarding. It should tell the member: "You are not behind. You are just missing a system."

Recommended scoring shape: 100 points across seven categories:

1. Knowledge foundation
2. Security and custody
3. Portfolio clarity
4. Goal alignment
5. Risk management
6. Trading discipline
7. Tax/scam awareness

Recommended result tracks:

- **Foundation Builder**: beginner or unsafe setup.
- **Systemless Holder**: owns crypto but no plan, allocation, thesis, or rules.
- **Portfolio Optimizer**: has holdings but needs goal-based allocation, security, and rebalancing.
- **Trading Discipline**: wants to trade, but must prove risk rules and journaling first.

## Red flag override gate

The assessment should include disqualifying red flags that route someone to Foundation Builder even if their raw score is high. Examples:

- No 2FA on primary exchange.
- Seed phrase stored in screenshots, notes app, email, or cloud drive.
- Uses leverage without a stop loss or position sizing rule.
- Has no idea what percentage of net worth is in crypto.
- Buys because someone said a coin will 100x.
- Has no transaction records for taxes.

This gate is product design, not legal padding. It protects users and the brand.

## Recommended paid workbook

**Discover Crypto Investor Operating System Workbook**

Promise: by the end, members have a written crypto investing system: goals, allocation, security setup, buy plan, sell/risk plan, trading rules if applicable, and monthly review cadence.

Suggested 10-module spine:

0. Start Here, No Hype
1. Your Crypto Starting Point
2. Secure Your Crypto Life
3. Define the Goal Before the Coin
4. Build Your Portfolio Buckets
5. The Buy Plan
6. The Sell, Hold, and Risk Plan
7. Trading Discipline, Optional Advanced Path
8. Taxes, Tracking, and Records
9. Scam and Red Flag Defense
10. Monthly Crypto Review

## Portfolio bucket framework

Use educational, illustrative buckets rather than personalized advice:

- Core bucket: BTC/ETH or highest-conviction long-term assets.
- Growth bucket: large/mid-cap assets with thesis.
- Speculative bucket: small caps, narratives, higher-risk opportunities.
- Stable reserve: stablecoins/cash for volatility, opportunities, or reducing systemic exposure.
- Trading bucket: separate capital for active trading only if rules are met.

All allocation examples must be clearly marked illustrative, not personalized financial advice.

## Trading stance

Include trading as a self-audit and optional advanced path, but do not make the core workbook "how to trade." Trading must be behind a discipline gate:

- Separate trading capital from investing capital.
- Define max risk per trade.
- Write the thesis before entering.
- Define invalidation before entering.
- Define take-profit levels before entering.
- Journal every trade.
- Review mistakes weekly.

## Compliance stance

Use plain-language trust-building risk language:

- Educational only.
- No personalized investment advice.
- No guaranteed results.
- Examples are illustrative.
- Crypto can lose significant value.
- Tax section routes to a tax professional.

Compliance should be woven into the product, not only footnoted.

## Skool community integration

Recommended channels around the workbook:

1. Start Here: assessment + workbook download.
2. Introductions: members post score band and track.
3. Portfolio Plan Lab: members post goal and bucket allocation, not exact dollar amounts if privacy matters.
4. Security Checkpoint: members confirm security basics.
5. Wins Wall: first completed workbook, first secure wallet setup, first written plan, first avoided FOMO buy.
6. Trade Journal Review: advanced only, no signal spam.
7. Ask Nick/Drew: weekly workbook questions.
8. Monthly Review Thread: recurring accountability habit.
