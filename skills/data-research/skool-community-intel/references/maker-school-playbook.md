## The 10 plays (ranked by his own 80/20)

1. **Show up every day.** 359/365 show-up rate, 100–250+ daily activities,
   ~60K activities over ~300 days = $300K/mo. 8/10 owners don't show up daily.
   Compounds: lower churn, goodwill (upsell later), 24/7 read on member problems,
   peer-helping culture.
2. **B2C → specific-outcome promise.** "learn AI" → "get your first paying customer
   in 90 days or your money back." Sell the measurable monetary outcome.
3. **Launch first, build later.** Pre-commit by paying the fee, record 1–2 lessons,
   launch, build the rest from member feedback.
4. **0.022 min/member/day math.** Power law (~1% make ~80% of posts). Solo scales
   to ~8,000 members before needing help.
5. **Batch everything.** All posts once/24h in one sitting, category by category
   (kill context-switching). Voice-to-text (Aqua/WhisperFlow) ~4x typing throughput
   + more authentic. 24h delay → members answer each other; he drops praise.
6. **Forums > calls.** Text posts serve ~100% + evergreen + AI-indexable; calls
   serve ~10–20% and decay. Went 1 call/month for most of the life. Async Loom
   videos replace ad-hoc help (self-select, 1.5x, evergreen library).
7. **Wins channel = most important category.** Constantly-populated wall of member
   results breaks limiting beliefs. Pin to front page; 2 of 3 pins = wins; "Best of"
   thread = standalone testimonial/sales asset.
8. **Onboarding = relationships + instant ROI.** Intro post + comment on 3 + read
   wins + start pre-program. Early comments build PEER micro-relationships =
   stickiness. $21K tool-discount aggregator = immediate ROI dwarfing the price.
9. **Pricing: start low-ish, raise consistently.** Started ~$20 (too cheap, drew
   tire-kickers; wishes ~$50–60), raised to ~$500 cap as the recorded-call asset
   library grew. Gradual increases reduce churn. Sidebar shows current higher price.
10. **Drip-feed classroom + ignore distractions.** Lock future months until tenure
    (beginners need action, not more knowledge). Ignore SaaS/partnership pitches —
    do the core thing 9% better.
