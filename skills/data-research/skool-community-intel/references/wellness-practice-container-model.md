# Wellness Skool: practice-container model

Use this when advising a wellness, breathwork, spiritual coaching, nervous-system regulation, recovery, or holistic-health creator on a Skool community.

## Core diagnosis

A wellness Skool underperforms when it is built as "course modules + general discussion." The community should be a **guided practice container** around the course, not just a place where the course lives.

For wellness creators, the asset is usually not proprietary information. The asset is embodied trust, daily practice, felt shifts, and member witness. Design the Skool so members practice, report what shifted, ask questions, and see other people shifting too.

## Positioning pattern

Frame the offer as a specific transformation/container, not a generic brand group.

Good shapes:
- "8-Week Nervous System Reset"
- "Be Here Now: The Nervous System Reset Community"
- "A guided community for learning how to feel safe in your body, regulate your nervous system, and build a daily practice that actually sticks."

Avoid:
- "Sean's course"
- "All Heart Holistic group"
- "Holistic healing community" unless paired with a concrete outcome

## Recommended channel architecture

Keep the first version simple. Too many channels kills early engagement.

1. **Start Here**
   - Welcome video
   - How the community works
   - Baseline self-assessment
   - Intro prompt: what brought you here, what regulation looks like, what you want to feel less controlled by

2. **Daily Reset**
   - The heartbeat of the community
   - Prompt: before-practice nervous-system score 1-10, practice used, after-practice score 1-10, one sentence on what shifted
   - Converts abstract wellness into visible proof

3. **The Pillars / Classroom**
   - Course modules live here
   - This is the library, not the life of the group

4. **Wins / Shifts**
   - Wellness version of a Wins channel
   - Name options: Regulation Wins, Small Shifts, I Came Back to Myself
   - Examples: paused before reacting, slept better, used the 2x breath before a hard conversation, HRV improved, felt safe in body
   - Becomes the sales asset later

5. **Ask [Creator]**
   - One place for questions
   - Creator answers async in batches to protect time

6. **Practice Anywhere / Breathwork Anywhere**
   - UGC challenge showing members practicing in ordinary life: car, office, airport, trail, Buc-ee's, kid's soccer practice
   - Makes the practice portable and shareable

7. **Live Session Vault**
   - Replays, workshop recordings, pop-up breathwork sessions, clipped mini-practices
   - Compounds all raw recordings into reusable community value

## Weekly rhythm

Give the community a repeatable cadence so members know why to return.

- Monday: intention/reset prompt
- Tuesday: live or recorded practice
- Wednesday: practice check-in / what has been hardest to sit with
- Friday: regulation wins / small shifts
- Sunday: stillness, reflection, nature, journaling

## Offer ladder

Best launch pattern:
1. **8-Week Nervous System Reset** as founding cohort: clean, finite, easier to sell.
2. Convert graduates into ongoing **Be Here Now Membership** or similar recurring community.
3. Later expansion: practitioner path / facilitator certification / partner track.

## High-leverage assets

- **2-Minute Reset Library:** morning reset, anxiety reset, pre-sleep reset, before hard conversation, after conflict, in the car, at work, in public.
- **7-Day Come Home Challenge:** one day per practice category, with after-practice shift posts.
- **Ripple Effect posts:** members share who they taught/helped and how the room changed.
- **Founder Circle:** first 50-100 members get a badge/locked-in price and help co-create the safest corner of the internet for the topic.

## Pitfalls

- Do not overbuild channels before there is behavior to organize.
- Do not make the classroom the whole product.
- Do not lead with a broad spiritual/wellness label when a concrete regulation/outcome promise exists.
- Do not over-index on live calls. Forum + repeatable daily practice beats a heavy call calendar for retention and creator leverage.
- Do not wait until every module is perfect. Launch a founding cohort and improve weekly.
